# Montage Demo — Sales Script

A suggested script for walking a prospect through the demo live, or for them
to self-guide.

## Opening

> "Instead of telling you what Eso Es does, let's show you — inside a
> business just like yours. Pick an industry."

Let the prospect choose their own industry if one matches (or the closest
fit). The fictional businesses are deliberately generic local trades so the
parallel to the prospect's own business is obvious.

## Step 1 — Ripple (Discovery)

> "This is what a customer searching for [service] sees. Notice the review
> score, the clear services list, and a direct call button. Ripple's job is
> to make sure your business looks like this everywhere it's found."

Click **Call Now** to move on.

## Step 2 — Gotcha (Missed call)

> "In real life, calls get missed — you're on a ladder, mid-haircut, on
> another job. Watch what happens next."

Point out the missed-call banner, then the instant auto-SMS. Let the
prospect pick an enquiry type themselves — this is the interactive moment
that makes the demo feel real rather than scripted.

> "Gotcha didn't just notice the call was missed — it kept the conversation
> going."

## Step 3 — Lead capture

> "That single enquiry is now a structured lead: what they need, how
> urgent it is, what it's roughly worth, and what your team should do next.
> No manual triage required."

## Step 4 — W'SUP (Playbook)

> "Here's the exact checklist your team would see for this enquiry type.
> Every staff member gets the same standard, every time — not whatever they
> remember from training six months ago."

Note: always mention this is an illustrative workflow, not a certified
safety or compliance document — especially for the electrician fault
scenario.

## Step 5 — Follow-up (Frother / Ripple)

For barber/cafe:
> "Frother brings them back automatically — a loyalty nudge or a
> well-timed reminder, not a generic blast."

For builder/electrician:
> "Once the job's done, Ripple asks for the review or referral at exactly
> the right moment — when satisfaction is highest."

## Step 6 — Results

> "That's one missed call, fully recovered, qualified, actioned, and
> followed up — without anyone manually doing any of it. Here's the
> estimated value of that one enquiry."

## Close

> "This is one enquiry. Imagine this running on every missed call, every
> week. Want us to do an Ops Check on your business and show you what this
> looks like with your real numbers?"

Use the **Book an Ops Check** or **Build This For My Business** buttons —
both open a pre-filled email to `oi@esoes.com.au`.

## Objection handling notes

- *"Is this actually live?"* — No, this is a frontend simulation with no
  backend; no real messages are sent. Be upfront about this immediately if
  asked — see `future-live-integrations.md` for what a real deployment
  would use.
- *"Are these real reviews / real numbers?"* — No, all data is clearly
  labelled as fictional demonstration data throughout the app.
